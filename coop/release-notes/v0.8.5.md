First-person spectating release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.7.0 to v0.8.4.** The co-op protocol version stays at 17, so v0.8.5 players can play with anyone on v0.7.0 or later. Players on v0.6.15 or older can't play with v0.7.0 or later and must update. Players on v0.7.1 to v0.8.4 are asked when they open the game; players on v0.6.13 to v0.7.0 are asked by the game as before; players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab ("Update to v0.8.5 - Install", or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub (`GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.5-win32.zip`). Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

Who needs v0.8.5: the player who is spectating. To see their partner's sight and zoom, and to follow their partner's view exactly up and down, the partner they watch needs v0.8.5 too. Watching a partner on an older version still shows their gun, shots and reloads.

## Story co-op

- **Spectate in first person.** When you're out of lives and watching a partner, you now see what they see, with their gun in your hands. It fires, flashes, kicks back, drops casings, reloads and switches guns when they do. Grenade throws are shown too. Your crosshair sits where theirs is, and when they aim you get their sight and zoom. The view now follows where they're looking up and down, not where their crosshair is.
- It's only for show: no bullets, ammo or noise come from your side, and guards don't hear it. When you respawn or get revived, you get your own gun and ammo back as they were.
- The free camera still has no gun. Press Fire to go back to your partner's view, gun and all.

## Known issues

- First-person spectating hasn't been tried with two guns at once, the rocket launcher, 3 or 4 players, a real gamepad, or a partner on an older version.
- The chat beep's sound and the give hint's button name on a real gamepad haven't been checked yet, and the new touches haven't been tried with 3 or 4 players.
- A tall stack of notifications can still reach the give hint under the crosshair.
- Credit for a kill or break made before a partner joined the mission still goes by the old rule (whoever acted last). A guard killed by another guard's bullet still counts as the host's, as it does on the stats screen.
- The grenade landing fix came late in testing. The objective checks for Control, Caverns, Jungle, Depot and Facility passed before it went in and weren't run again after.
- The START button hasn't been tried on a host with a campaign to continue, in a lobby of 3 or 4 players, or with a real gamepad yet.
- The knockback fix was checked by watching the body stay put, not by going down on Facility's stairs themselves. If you still get stuck after a revive, an F8 report helps.
- The Facility chat fix was tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows or in a real Steam game.
- At HUD Scale High and 640x480, a 50% Notification Size is too small to read.
- The colour picker hasn't been tried with a real gamepad yet.
- The Spanish accent fix hasn't been checked on real Windows yet. If you still see gaps in Spanish words, an F8 report helps. Accents in Windows system boxes (such as the update box) haven't been checked on real Windows either.
- Adding the language files after an in-game update from v0.7.1 or older has been checked with a test of the updater, not yet with a real update. If Spanish isn't in the Language list after you update, unzip `GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.5-win32.zip` over your game folder; your saves, settings and cart image are kept.
- The update box and its progress window were tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows. If the box doesn't show, the CO-OP screen still offers the update.
- The fix for a game splitting in two after a long dropout has been tested on our simulated network, not yet in many real Steam games. If you still end up in two separate games after a dropout, an F8 report from both players helps.
- The crash-safe saves and the updater's resume were tested on Linux. Their Windows-only parts haven't had a run on real Windows yet.
- In Bunker 2 a joiner's punches have been reported missing the cell guard; we haven't reproduced it yet. F8 reports help.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
