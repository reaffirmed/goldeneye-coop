Online versus release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Everyone must update: v0.6.7 does not play with v0.6.6 or older.** The co-op protocol version goes from 15 to 16 (versus now plays the host's rules, and a player can leave a match without ending it), so a v0.6.6 player can't join a v0.6.7 host or the other way round. This applies to co-op as well as versus. Players on v0.6.6 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.7 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab), and trying to join a v0.6.7 host offers it at once. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## New in online versus

- **Chat, toasts and the weapon menu.** Chat, toasts, the pad chat keyboard, Sound Captions, the TALK indicator and the weapon menu now show in versus. Before, they were only drawn in co-op. The weapon menu and number keys work there too, except for the player holding the flag in Flag Tag, who can't switch weapons, as in the original game. The reconnecting panel also shows in versus.
- **The host's rules for everyone.** Every PC used to apply its own Multiplayer Options (Invisibility, DK, Tiny, Paintball, No Radar, Turbo, Unlimited Ammo, No Armor, Spawn with Weapon, Random Spawns), cheat picks and button codes, so players could end up playing by different rules. An online match now uses only the host's rules. The host has two new rows under Rules: **Radar** and **Body Armour**.
- **Leaving a match no longer ends it.** A joiner who quits now leaves on their own, and everyone else plays on. A player who quits, or whose game stops responding for 2 seconds, disappears from the arena and the radar and drops the flag or the Golden Gun, and a "LEFT THE MATCH" message shows. The match ends once only one player or team is left. If the host quits, the match still ends for everyone.

## Fixed

- **Other players' bodies no longer hop in versus,** and they move smoothly above 60 fps. These are the same fixes v0.6.4 made for co-op partners.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
