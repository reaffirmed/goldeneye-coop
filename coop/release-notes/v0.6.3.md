Statue fix release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.5.0 to v0.6.2.** The co-op protocol version stays at 14, so v0.6.3 players can still play with anyone on v0.5.x or v0.6.x. The fix runs on the host, so hosts should update. Players on v0.6.2, v0.6.1, v0.6.0, v0.5.x, v0.4.0 or v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.3 - Install". Players on v0.4.0 or older must update to play with v0.5.x or later. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Fixed

- **Statue: Janus's guards no longer open fire because of a partner who isn't there.** In v0.6.2, a partner waiting somewhere else (at the helicopter, for example) counted as walking away from the meeting and set off the ambush as soon as it began, and a partner holding a gun anywhere did the same. Now only players who come to the statue count. The ambush still goes off if one of them walks back out or has a gun out, and if nobody is left at the statue it goes off as it does when Bond leaves in single player.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
