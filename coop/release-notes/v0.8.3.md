Fixes release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.7.0 to v0.8.2.** The co-op protocol version stays at 17, so v0.8.3 players can play with anyone on v0.7.0 or later. Players on v0.6.15 or older can't play with v0.7.0 or later and must update. Players on v0.7.1 to v0.8.2 are asked when they open the game; players on v0.6.13 to v0.7.0 are asked by the game as before; players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab ("Update to v0.8.3 - Install", or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub (`GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.3-win32.zip`). Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

Who needs v0.8.3: the objective credit fix works when the host is on v0.8.3. The grenade fix is on each player's own game.

## Story co-op

- **Objective credit goes to the player who did it.** Testers said the game still sometimes named the wrong player for an objective. The host used to credit whoever had last pressed something or fired a shot, so three cases went wrong:
  - A joiner's grenade or mine that finished a "destroy" objective was credited to whoever fired last during the fuse, usually the host.
  - Someone who pressed a console, switch or alarm box a few seconds earlier took the credit from the player who broke the last target.
  - Objectives that check for a kill or a broken object (such as Xenia on Jungle and Silo's objects) went to the latest shooter, not the killer.

  Now the host keeps track of who broke each object and who killed each character, grenades, mines and explosions included. A "destroy" objective credits whoever broke its last target.
- **Grenades land where they should.** On PC, a thrown item's height could come from leftover memory, so a grenade could land up to about 2 m away from where it should. Fixed.
- The Dam modem, Frigate tracker bug and Surface 2 mine no longer fail the team when one player misses with theirs. That fix came in v0.8.0 and needs every player on v0.8.0 or later.

## Known issues

- Credit for a kill or break made before a partner joined the mission still goes by the old rule (whoever acted last). A guard killed by another guard's bullet still counts as the host's, as it does on the stats screen.
- The grenade landing fix came late in testing. The objective checks for Control, Caverns, Jungle, Depot and Facility passed before it went in and weren't run again after.
- The START button hasn't been tried on a host with a campaign to continue, in a lobby of 3 or 4 players, or with a real gamepad yet.
- The knockback fix was checked by watching the body stay put, not by going down on Facility's stairs themselves. If you still get stuck after a revive, an F8 report helps.
- The Facility chat fix was tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows or in a real Steam game.
- At HUD Scale High and 640x480, a 50% Notification Size is too small to read.
- The colour picker hasn't been tried with a real gamepad yet.
- The Spanish accent fix hasn't been checked on real Windows yet. If you still see gaps in Spanish words, an F8 report helps. Accents in Windows system boxes (such as the update box) haven't been checked on real Windows either.
- Adding the language files after an in-game update from v0.7.1 or older has been checked with a test of the updater, not yet with a real update. If Spanish isn't in the Language list after you update, unzip `GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.3-win32.zip` over your game folder; your saves, settings and cart image are kept.
- The update box and its progress window were tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows. If the box doesn't show, the CO-OP screen still offers the update.
- The fix for a game splitting in two after a long dropout has been tested on our simulated network, not yet in many real Steam games. If you still end up in two separate games after a dropout, an F8 report from both players helps.
- The crash-safe saves and the updater's resume were tested on Linux. Their Windows-only parts haven't had a run on real Windows yet.
- In Bunker 2 a joiner's punches have been reported missing the cell guard; we haven't reproduced it yet. F8 reports help.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
