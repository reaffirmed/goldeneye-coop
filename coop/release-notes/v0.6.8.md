Co-op fix release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.6.7.** The co-op protocol version stays at 16, so v0.6.8 players can still play with anyone on v0.6.7. Players on v0.6.6 or older can't play with v0.6.7 or v0.6.8 and must update. The fix only works when both the player who shoots the item and the other players are on v0.6.8, so everyone should update. Players on v0.6.7 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.8 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Fixed

- **A shot thrown or dropped item moves the same for every player.** When a player shot an item lying on the ground, it only bounced in that player's game, and everyone else kept seeing it where it first landed. On Dam, for example, the joiner could shoot the covert modem the host threw and only the joiner saw it move. Now every player sees the item fly the same way and come to rest in the same spot. This covers grenades, mines, the covert modem, the camera, the bomb case, plastique, the GoldenEye key and guns that guards drop, and also items knocked loose from what they were stuck to.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
