A fix for the Weapon Menu in split screen, on top of v0.9.0.

**Plays with v0.9.0.** The co-op protocol stays at 18, so v0.9.0 and v0.9.1 players can join each other's games. Only a PC with two or more players on its screen needs the fix. Players on v0.9.0 are offered the update when the game opens.

## Fixes

- **Split screen: each player has their own Weapon Menu.** In v0.9.0, all the players on a screen shared one Weapon Menu, the numbered slots along the top of the view. When one player opened it, it opened in every view, and the next shot from another player picked a weapon instead of firing. Each player's menu now opens only in their own view and only changes their own weapon. This affected split screen offline and online. Until you update, turning **Weapon Menu** off in the options avoids it. Needed by: any PC with more than one player on its screen.

## Known issues

- Split screen still hasn't been tried in a real Steam lobby or with real controllers. The fix was checked in the game offline, with two players on Dam and a test driver pressing the buttons, and by a new automated two-player test. F8 reports help.
- v0.9.1 wasn't played online against v0.9.0. The fix doesn't change anything sent over the network.
- The automated mission runs (every mission, two copies over a lossy link) didn't run for this release: CI still has no cart image to run them with.
- Only player 1 can skip the intro. Players on one screen share one Ready and one vote, and the stats page keeps stats for player 1 only.
- Partners on one screen start the mission close together.
- Versus stays one player per PC.
- Cradle guards out of place and an Aztec guard that wouldn't die were reported but not reproduced. F8 reports help.
- Statue's ending with Valentin as the character hasn't been looked at yet.
