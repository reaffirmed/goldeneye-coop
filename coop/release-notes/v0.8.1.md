Player colours release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.7.0 to v0.8.0.** The co-op protocol version stays at 17, so v0.8.1 players can play with anyone on v0.7.0 or later. Players on v0.6.15 or older can't play with v0.7.0 or later and must update. Players on v0.7.1 and v0.8.0 are asked when they open the game; players on v0.6.13 to v0.7.0 are asked by the game as before; players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab ("Update to v0.8.1 - Install", or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub (`GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.1-win32.zip`). Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

Who needs v0.8.1 for each change: your colour shows to the players who are on v0.8.1 too; a v0.8.0 player sees everyone in the usual colours, and is seen in them. The Spanish fix is on each player's own game.

## New: your own colour

- **Pick the colour the others see you in.** The co-op lobby has a new **Colour** row. Choosing it opens a colour picker over the lobby, drawn in the game's own look. It works with the mouse, the keyboard or a gamepad, and Escape (or B) closes it without changing anything. **OK** keeps your pick, **Cancel** keeps the old one, and **Default Colour** goes back to the usual colour for your player slot. An "In chat:" line shows how your name will look before you choose.
- **Your colour follows you around.** Every player sees your name and messages in chat, your stripe in the party list, your dot on the radar and your pings in your colour.
- **Colour Blind still wins.** If you have **Colour Blind** on, you see its colours for everyone, whatever they picked. The picker says so when it applies.
- Your pick is kept in `midas.ini` (`coop_color` under `[display]`). The picker is made with Dear ImGui, which is credited in the third-party notices. Its text is in Spanish too.

## Spanish

- **Accented letters show on Windows.** On Windows, letters such as the "ó" in "ratón" and "Misión" were drawn as gaps in the game's text. Fixed.

## Known issues

- The colour picker hasn't been tried with a real gamepad yet, only with the mouse and keyboard.
- The accent fix hasn't been checked on real Windows yet. If you still see gaps in Spanish words, an F8 report helps. Accents in Windows system boxes (such as the update box) haven't been checked on real Windows either.
- Adding the language files after an in-game update from v0.7.1 or older has been checked with a test of the updater, not yet with a real update. If Spanish isn't in the Language list after you update, unzip `GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.1-win32.zip` over your game folder; your saves, settings and cart image are kept.
- The update box and its progress window were tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows. If the box doesn't show, the CO-OP screen still offers the update.
- The fix for a game splitting in two after a long dropout has been tested on our simulated network, not yet in many real Steam games. If you still end up in two separate games after a dropout, an F8 report from both players helps.
- The crash-safe saves and the updater's resume were tested on Linux. Their Windows-only parts haven't had a run on real Windows yet.
- In Bunker 2 a joiner's punches have been reported missing the cell guard; we haven't reproduced it yet. F8 reports help.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
