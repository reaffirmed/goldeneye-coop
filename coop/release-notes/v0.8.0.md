Spanish language release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.7.0 and v0.7.1.** The co-op protocol version stays at 17, so v0.8.0 players can play with anyone on v0.7.0 or later. Players on v0.6.15 or older can't play with v0.7.0 or later and must update. Players on v0.7.1 are asked when they open the game; players on v0.6.13 to v0.7.0 are asked by the game as before; players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab ("Update to v0.8.0 - Install", or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub (`GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.0-win32.zip`). Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

Who needs v0.8.0 for each change: the silenced-gun fix works when the host is on v0.8.0. The Bunker 2 ending fix is for each joiner on v0.8.0. Spanish, Partner Fade strength and versus collision are on each player's own game. The one-each mission items work fully when every player is on v0.8.0.

## New: Spanish

- **Play the whole game in Spanish.** Pick **Port Options > Language** (also in the lobby's options); it takes effect the next time you start the game. The game's own text, its fonts and the images with words on them (such as the file screen's ELEGIR ARCHIVO and the folder stamps) come from Sogun and IlDucci's Spanish translation v2.0, applied to your own cart as the game starts. Nothing is written to your cart image. Everything the port adds is translated too: the settings, the co-op and versus screens, the HUD, toasts and prompts.
- **Each player picks their own language.** A host in Spanish and a joiner in English play the same game, each with their own text. Toasts, the kill feed and objective messages show in each player's language.
- **Updating from inside the game brings Spanish too.** The updater in v0.7.1 and older only replaces the kinds of files it already knew, so it leaves out the new `lang` folder. The first time v0.8.0 starts after such an update, it adds the missing language files itself from the download the update kept, so Spanish shows in the Language list with no extra download.
- The translation's readme ships with it in `lang/es/`, and it is credited in the third-party notices. `lang/README.md` explains how to add another language.

## Story co-op

- **Bunker 2: the escape's ending plays on the joiner's screen.** At the end of the Bunker 2 escape, a joiner could see the cutscene without Natalya, or have their mission end just as the host's cutscene started. The joiner's Bond now goes to the cutscene's spot, and the joiner's copy of the exit script waits for the host's ending, so Bond and Natalya run out to the helipad on every screen. Bunker 1, Surface 2 and Egyptian use the same kind of ending and the same fix, though only Bunker 2 has been played through with it.
- **One player's missed throw no longer fails the team.** On Dam, if one player threw the covert modem and missed, the whole mission failed, even while a partner still had theirs. A one-each item now fails its objective only once no player has one left to throw and none is still in the air. This covers the covert modem on Dam, the tracker bug on Frigate and the remote mine on Surface 2. Silo's plastique still starts the escape countdown as before. When a joiner plants the bug or the mine on the helicopter, they now get the credit and the toast, not the host.
- **Silenced guns are quiet again for partners.** Testers said suppressed guns didn't seem to work for other players, and guards seemed to know where everyone was. On a laggy connection, or when a partner's game ran slower than the host's, the host could count one of a partner's shots two to four times, so guards heard it from two to four times as far. A silenced PP7 could be as loud as an unsilenced one. Each shot now counts once, and guards hear every player as in the original game.
- **Partner Fade has a strength.** **Port Options > Partner Fade** is now a list: Off, then 25% to 200% in steps of 25. 100% is the fade you had before, and it stays the default. Below 100% a partner only fades part of the way (at 50% they are half see-through at their closest). Above 100% they fade out completely and from further away (at 200% they are gone at 2 m). A `midas.ini` with the old on/off setting reads as 100% or Off.

## Versus

- **Other players' bodies block you again.** Running at an opponent left you standing inside their body, because every other player's collision stayed at the middle of the map. Bodies now block each other as they do in split screen, with some give for lag so you don't get stuck on someone who was still moving. Two players sprinting straight at each other at full speed can still pass through.

## Known issues

- Adding the language files after an in-game update has been checked with a test of the updater, not yet with a real update. If Spanish isn't in the Language list after you update, unzip `GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.0-win32.zip` over your game folder; your saves, settings and cart image are kept.
- Accents in Windows system boxes (such as the update box) haven't been checked on real Windows yet. The game's own screens show them correctly.
- The update box and its progress window were tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows. If the box doesn't show, the CO-OP screen still offers the update.
- The fix for a game splitting in two after a long dropout has been tested on our simulated network, not yet in many real Steam games. If you still end up in two separate games after a dropout, an F8 report from both players helps.
- The crash-safe saves and the updater's resume were tested on Linux. Their Windows-only parts haven't had a run on real Windows yet.
- In Bunker 2 a joiner's punches have been reported missing the cell guard; we haven't reproduced it yet. F8 reports help.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
