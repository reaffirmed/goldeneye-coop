Split-screen release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Everyone must update: v0.9.0 does not play with v0.8.6 or older.** The co-op protocol version goes from 17 to 18 (a PC can now bring more than one player into a game), so a v0.8.x player can't join a v0.9.0 host or the other way round, in co-op or versus. Either side gets a "Different Versions" message if they try. Players on v0.7.1 to v0.8.6 are asked to update when the game opens; players on v0.6.13 to v0.7.0 are asked by the game as before; players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab ("Update to v0.9.0 - Install", or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub (`GoldenEye-Coop-v0.9.0-win32.zip`). Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Split screen in the story missions

- **Two to four players on one screen.** On the CO-OP screen, set **Local Players** to 2, 3 or 4 and pick a character for each extra player. Each player needs their own controller; player 1 can use the keyboard and mouse.
- **Offline or online.** **Play on This Screen** starts a split-screen game with nobody online, and works without Steam. In a Steam lobby the same rows bring your screen's players in, so a split-screen PC can host or join, up to four players in all. If the host has no room for all of yours, you see "Session Full".
- **The real missions.** Objectives, guards, the mission's own sky and fog, and the solo debrief all stay as in one-player story. Guards target every player, revives, respawning beside a partner, friendly fire and the radar work for players on the same screen just as for online partners, and the mission fails when everyone is down.
- **Each view has its own HUD.** The ammo sits in each view's bottom corner, the radar sits above the objectives, and the co-op text is drawn at half size so nothing is cut off.
- The intro and the ending play full screen. Whoever opens the pause watch gets the whole screen until they close it.
- The new lobby rows and the "Session Full" message are in Spanish too.

## Known issues

- Split screen hasn't been tried in a real Steam lobby or with real controllers yet. It was tested offline with 2, 3 and 4 players, and online over our simulated network with 2+1, 1+2 and 2+2 players on two PCs. F8 reports help.
- Only player 1 can skip the intro. Players on one screen share one Ready and one vote, and the stats page keeps stats for player 1 only.
- Partners on one screen start the mission close together.
- Versus stays one player per PC.
- The Spanish "Session Full" message was checked in the log, not on screen.
- Cradle guards out of place and an Aztec guard that wouldn't die were reported but not reproduced. F8 reports help.
- Statue's ending with Valentin as the character hasn't been looked at yet.
