Co-op fix release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.5.0 to v0.6.1.** The co-op protocol version stays at 14, so v0.6.2 players can still play with anyone on v0.5.x or v0.6.x. Most of these fixes run on the host, so updating is strongly recommended for hosts, and joiners need v0.6.2 for the joiner-side fixes. Players on v0.6.1, v0.6.0, v0.5.x, v0.4.0 or v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.2 - Install". Players on v0.4.0 or older must update to play with v0.5.x or later. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

These are the bugs found in two-player testing, where the mission scripts still assumed there is only one Bond.

## Fixed

### Guards and partners

- **Guards hit a partner as often as the host.** The partner's own body was stopping over half the guards' shots at a joiner. A partner also gets the same short pause after each hit that the host's Bond gets.
- **Every game shows the same guard faces.** Guards' heads now come from a roll every game starts the same way for the mission.
- **Partners' guns show in their hands.** A partner who drew a gun could keep the unarmed idle pose and seemed to hop now and then. Mirrored guards also move smoothly on the joiner.
- **Guards get back up on the joiner.** A guard the host had knocked down could stay lying on the joiner while still shooting at them (seen on Jungle). A guard that respawns now also appears as a new guard on the joiner, with its own head, gun and place.
- **Fewer "the chrs here are not the host's" errors.** Bodies are left out of the check that compares the two games, so guards dying fast after explosions no longer read as being out of step.

### Missions

- **Dam:** a joiner who takes the bungee jump first no longer fails the mission.
- **Facility and Train:** a joiner no longer sees an objective flash as failed when it hasn't.
- **Frigate:** an ending the joiner reaches first now plays on both games together.
- **Bunker 1:** the six-door gate no longer floods the connection with door events.
- **Statue:**
  - Natalya appears and her objective no longer fails, even when the host can see where she spawns.
  - Janus stays put instead of wandering between players.
  - The ambush starts when any player walks away from the unmasking, and holds off only while every player has their gun put away, as in single player.
  - A partner's shot counts as Bond shooting at a guard, and guards looking for help never pick a partner.
  - The Janus slap and the Cradle "hurt" effect no longer show a flinch on the joiner that never happens on the host.
- **Jungle:** Natalya no longer attacks the joiner.
- **Train:** objects a script makes breakable also break on the joiner, and explosions show on both games. The host's people also move on the joiner during cutscenes.

### Mission end, vote and spectating

- **Vote screen:** voters' names share the row instead of overlapping. A joiner whose mission ends first waits for the host's vote, and a player already back in the lobby no longer counts as a voter.
- **Ending the mission while down:** a player out of lives can now press Start, then Start again, to end the mission. A host with partners ends it for everyone, and anyone else leaves.
- **Spectating:** a player who is down sees their own body where they fell, and the free camera and photo mode fly through walls instead of catching on them.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
