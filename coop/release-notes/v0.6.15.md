Co-op features release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.6.7 to v0.6.14.** The co-op protocol version stays at 16, so v0.6.15 players can still play with anyone on v0.6.7 or later. Players on v0.6.6 or older can't play with v0.6.7 or later and must update. Players on v0.6.13 and v0.6.14 are asked to update when the game opens; players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab ("Update to v0.6.15 - Install", or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip. The escort and guard fixes work when the host is on v0.6.15; the versus HUD change is on each player's own screen.

## Story co-op

- **Escorts stay with one player.** Natalya and the other escorts could switch between players from one run to the next when nobody was close. Once an escort runs or walks to a player, she now keeps following that player. She moves on only when that player goes down or leaves, when her own script picks someone else (such as "Bond within 10 m"), or when another player has been much nearer for 3 seconds while she can't reach her leader.
- **Nearest player first.** With 3 or 4 players, when an escort's or guard's check fails for its own player, the others are now tried nearest first instead of in player order.
- **Big fights no longer freeze everyone.** The original game stops every moving character once 10 guards have spotted Bond in the last 2 seconds. Guards fighting each partner counted toward that, so co-op hit the limit far sooner and escorts and guards could stand still mid-fight. Each player in the mission now gets the solo budget of 10. Solo and versus are unchanged.

## Versus

- **No hit marker or hit direction arc.** The X round the crosshair when you land a hit and the red arc pointing toward whoever hit you are gone from the versus HUD. The kill feed and the red flash on taking damage stay.

## Known issues

- In Bunker 2 a joiner's punches have been reported missing the cell guard; we haven't reproduced it yet. F8 reports help.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
