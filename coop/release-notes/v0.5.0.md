Feature and fix release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Everyone must update: v0.5.0 does not play with v0.4.0 or older.** The co-op protocol version goes from 13 to 14 (the host's mission countdown now rides the world packet, and versus shares pickups, drops, throws and object damage), so a v0.4.0 player can't join a v0.5.0 host or the other way round. Players on v0.4.0 or v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.5.0 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab), and trying to join a v0.5.0 host offers it at once. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## New

- **Smarter guard AI (optional).** A new Guard AI Port Option, also on the host's Game Setup under Player Scaling. **Classic** (the default) keeps the original guards. **Smarter** guards investigate what they hear, call nearby guards for help, sometimes run for an alarm, fire in bursts, take cover and peek, flank or advance when they lose sight of you, fall back when badly hurt, notice bodies, and spread out over the players in co-op. In co-op the host's setting decides. Smarter runs still count for cheats and team times.
- **Online versus plays properly.** Other players now run, strafe, crouch and face where they are really going and looking (before, they could stand in an idle pose facing their spawn). Arena weapon, ammo and armour pickups are shared and respawn together on every game; guns a dying player drops, thrown grenades and mines (with kill credit for the thrower), object damage and knocks reach everyone.

## Fixes

- **Statue: a joiner can holster for Janus.** Janus's "no gun" check only read the host's hands, so a joiner holstering failed objective B. NPC checks for the gun Bond is holding and Bond's health now ask the player in question, and escorts such as Natalya no longer pop into view in front of a joiner.
- **Guards react to a joiner's near misses.** A joiner's shots that went past a guard now startle it, as the host's always did. Civilians that step beside or behind Bond use the right player's facing, and a joiner can no longer be hurt or pick things up during an ending the host's script started.
- **Facility: Dr. Doak's decoder** now reaches the team when the host is down or out of lives (before, objective B completed but the decoder went to nobody). A host who is down no longer counts as Bond for a level's room and distance checks while a partner stands.
- **Cradle: one player falling no longer fails the mission.** A fall into the shaft is now an ordinary death for that player, so lives, bleed-out and the team-out rule decide what happens next.
- **Mission countdowns are the host's.** Silo, Statue, Bunker 2 and the other timed missions now run on the host's clock for everyone, including late joiners, and the final blast goes off for everyone at once.
- **Host migration no longer splits 3-4 player groups.** A bug made every joiner look gone for a moment every half second, so a host lost at the wrong moment left each joiner hosting alone. Player counts, Player Scaling, votes and voice no longer flicker with it.
- **Far-away players can drop in and stay.** A joiner on a long or patchy line now waits out a short drop instead of leaving the game to host alone; reliable messages are no longer flooded over long lines; and the host sends each joiner much less with 3 or more players.
- **Votes count only players still here.** A player who crashes or leaves stops holding up the after-mission and cutscene-skip votes after 3 s, and votes follow their players through a host migration.
- **The updater explains a folder it can't write to** ("Can't write to the game's folder; move it somewhere you own") instead of "download failed".
- **From the tester report:** level music comes back after you die; the mouse steers the spectator free camera (with Look Inverted, smart mouse lock, and strafe the right way round, also in photo mode), and a pad resting slightly off centre no longer drifts it; you hear from the camera while spectating; chat lines can be longer, word-wrap in a mission and no longer freeze after a cutscene; objective credit goes to the player who pressed Use; a joiner's pickup counts at once while the host is down; hostage-takers' shots kill hostages on high co-op difficulty; and guards armed by their script (Statue, Trevelyan's ambush) hold their guns on a joiner's screen too.
- Several maths functions in co-op code returned garbage because they were never declared; this affected the check for which guard you are aiming at and give-to-partner's height check.

## Known issues

- **The host's rockets still hurt joiners with Friendly Fire off.** Blasts from the host's own launcher rounds aren't tagged with their owner yet.
- In Statue, Natalya may only get up after a death, and the ending can then show INCOMPLETE when the helicopter countdown runs out.
- A drone gun can point different ways on the host's and a joiner's screen; the damage each player takes is still right.
