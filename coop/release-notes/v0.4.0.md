Feature release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Everyone must update: v0.4.0 does not play with v0.3.0 or older.** The co-op protocol version goes from 12 to 13 (new Game Setup options and new data in hits, explosions and stats), so a v0.3.0 player can't join a v0.4.0 host or the other way round. Players on v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.4.0 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab), and trying to join a v0.4.0 host offers it at once. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## New

- **Player Scaling.** A new Game Setup option under Guards: Auto (default), Off, 3 Players or 4 Players. With 3 or 4 players, guards get more health, better aim, harder hits, quicker reactions, and there are more of them, on top of the Guards setting. Auto follows the player count during the mission: when someone drops in, guards toughen after a few seconds; when someone leaves, they ease off again (extra guards already placed stay).
- **Friendly Fire levels.** Friendly Fire is now Off (default), Reduced (half damage) or Full, and it covers grenades, mines and rockets as well as bullets. The host's setting decides for everyone. A partner who is already down can't be hurt. When Friendly Fire is on, the team results page shows each player's **Friendly hits**.

## Fixes

- **Security cameras see joiners.** Before, only the host could set off a camera, so a joiner could stand in front of one forever. Now a camera that sees any player raises the alarm for everyone.
- **NPCs no longer get stuck inside players.** A guard, hostage or Boris walking into a joiner could wedge both of them so neither could move (Frigate hostages, Boris in Bunker 2). Overlapping bodies now let each other go until they are apart, and a downed player no longer blocks anyone.
- **Guards react to every player.** Guards busy with something else (Frigate's hostage-takers, Boris and others on the move) only ever watched whoever was nearest when the mission started, so they could ignore a joiner right in front of them. They now check every player for sight, aim and distance. Guards also hear a joiner's gunfire now, not only the host's.
- **Text no longer cut off on the CO-OP screen.** Long chat lines wrap instead of ending in "...", the description along the foot of the screen gets a second line, and long names scroll when picked. A partner's ping moved under their name to give the name more room. "Next Player Hosts" is now "New Host" and "Controller Layout" is "Button Layout" so they fit.
- With 3 or 4 players, a joiner's shots at a guard before the first sync no longer predict a kill too early.

## Known issues

- **The host's rockets still hurt joiners with Friendly Fire off.** Blasts from the host's own launcher rounds (rocket launcher, grenade launcher) aren't tagged with their owner yet, so joiners take full damage from them whatever the Friendly Fire setting. Grenades and mines thrown or planted by the host, and every joiner's blasts, follow the setting.
- A drone gun can point different ways on the host's and a joiner's screen; the damage each player takes is still right.
