Second release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Everyone must update.** The co-op protocol version goes from 11 to 12, so a v0.2.0 game can't play with a v0.1.0 one. Every player needs this same zip and their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## New

- **Lives, respawn and bleed-out rules.** The host sets three more rules under When Down, and every player gets them:
  - Lives: Unlimited (default), 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 per player per mission. Each down costs one. Going down on the last life puts you out until the mission ends, as bleeding out does, and partners see OUT and can't revive you.
  - Respawn: 3-20 s (the old rule, still the default) or a fixed 5 to 90 s.
  - Bleed Out (Revive only): 15 s to 3 min, 60 s by default.
- **Cutscenes show your own character.** In the intro and in scripted cinemas such as the Dam bungee jump, each player sees only the character they picked; partners are hidden. A cinema a partner set off moves your Bond onto the star's spot, so the Dam jump plays for the joiner too.
- **CO-OP screen in the style of Modern Warfare 2.** Tabs for PLAY / LOBBY, GAME SETUP, FIND GAMES / INVITE, CONTROLS and CHAT; a list where every value steps both ways with Left / Right; a party panel with each player's head, name and status beside the next mission's photo; a line that explains the picked row; and button prompts for the device you last used. Change tabs with LB / RB, `,` / `.` or a click, and press T for chat.

## Fixes from the v0.1.0 play tests

- Your own Bond in the intro swirl and cinemas wears your lobby character.
- A partner's shot on a guard draws a smoke puff, not red sparks.
- The level music comes back after a revive or respawn.
- A talking partner's speaker icon at the screen's left edge no longer turns into a bar across the screen.
- Versus players move smoothly between updates instead of jittering.

## Mission objective fixes

- **Bunker 2** (and every exit script): one player's death no longer stops the exit for good; only a team wipe counts as Bond dead.
- **Frigate**: a joiner's bomb defuser counts for the team.
- Objectives a joiner completes no longer flicker or briefly fail.
- **Surface 2**: a partner drawing or throwing the mine is no longer taken for the mine in the level, and a planted mine sticks to the helicopter on every screen.
- **Runway**: the tank is in sync for everyone (position, heading and turret), and only one player can drive it.
- **Dam**: tower guards no longer freeze for joiners.

## Build and CI

- The Linux and Windows CI apt installs now time out and retry instead of hanging on a stalled mirror.
