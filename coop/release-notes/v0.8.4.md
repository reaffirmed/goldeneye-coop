Co-op polish release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.7.0 to v0.8.3.** The co-op protocol version stays at 17, so v0.8.4 players can play with anyone on v0.7.0 or later. Players on v0.6.15 or older can't play with v0.7.0 or later and must update. Players on v0.7.1 to v0.8.3 are asked when they open the game; players on v0.6.13 to v0.7.0 are asked by the game as before; players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab ("Update to v0.8.4 - Install", or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub (`GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.4-win32.zip`). Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

Who needs v0.8.4: everything in it is on each player's own screen, so you get it as soon as you update, whatever your partners run.

## Story co-op

- **Name tags dim behind walls.** A partner's name tag fades to a dimmer look when a wall or a closed door is between you, and brightens again when they come into sight. A REVIVE tag always stays bright.
- **See when a partner gets hit.** When a partner loses health or armour, the lost piece of their bar in the party list stays pale for a moment before it shrinks away, and the bar's frame flashes red.
- **A beep for chat.** A partner's chat line now plays a short beep (your own lines don't). Set how loud it is with the new **Chat Sound** slider in **Options > Audio** (also on the watch's Audio page), from 0 to 200%; all the way down turns it off. It's `chat_volume` under `[display]` in `midas.ini`.
- **Repeated notifications merge.** The same notification from the same player, while it's still on screen, comes back once with "X2", "X3" instead of stacking up. Giving ammo several times in a row shows one running total ("GAVE 81 ROUNDS TO ..."), not one notification per give.
- **A hint for giving ammo.** When you look at a partner up close and a press would give them ammo, "G: GIVE AMMO TO NAME" shows under the crosshair, with the button of whichever device you used last. It stays hidden while the revive prompt, the weapon menu or chat is up, or while you're spectating.
- **A timer bar on the REVIVE tag.** A downed partner's REVIVE tag now has a red bar under it that drains with the time left before they bleed out (Revive) or come back anyway (Revive or Timer). Under Revive the tag pulses faster as time runs low: from 10 seconds, then faster again from 5. It stops pulsing while someone is reviving them.
- All the new text is in Spanish too.

## Known issues

- The chat beep's sound and the give hint's button name on a real gamepad haven't been checked yet, and the new touches haven't been tried with 3 or 4 players.
- A tall stack of notifications can still reach the give hint under the crosshair.
- Credit for a kill or break made before a partner joined the mission still goes by the old rule (whoever acted last). A guard killed by another guard's bullet still counts as the host's, as it does on the stats screen.
- The grenade landing fix came late in testing. The objective checks for Control, Caverns, Jungle, Depot and Facility passed before it went in and weren't run again after.
- The START button hasn't been tried on a host with a campaign to continue, in a lobby of 3 or 4 players, or with a real gamepad yet.
- The knockback fix was checked by watching the body stay put, not by going down on Facility's stairs themselves. If you still get stuck after a revive, an F8 report helps.
- The Facility chat fix was tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows or in a real Steam game.
- At HUD Scale High and 640x480, a 50% Notification Size is too small to read.
- The colour picker hasn't been tried with a real gamepad yet.
- The Spanish accent fix hasn't been checked on real Windows yet. If you still see gaps in Spanish words, an F8 report helps. Accents in Windows system boxes (such as the update box) haven't been checked on real Windows either.
- Adding the language files after an in-game update from v0.7.1 or older has been checked with a test of the updater, not yet with a real update. If Spanish isn't in the Language list after you update, unzip `GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.4-win32.zip` over your game folder; your saves, settings and cart image are kept.
- The update box and its progress window were tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows. If the box doesn't show, the CO-OP screen still offers the update.
- The fix for a game splitting in two after a long dropout has been tested on our simulated network, not yet in many real Steam games. If you still end up in two separate games after a dropout, an F8 report from both players helps.
- The crash-safe saves and the updater's resume were tested on Linux. Their Windows-only parts haven't had a run on real Windows yet.
- In Bunker 2 a joiner's punches have been reported missing the cell guard; we haven't reproduced it yet. F8 reports help.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
