Co-op features release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.6.7 to v0.6.13.** The co-op protocol version stays at 16, so v0.6.14 players can still play with anyone on v0.6.7 or later. Players on v0.6.6 or older can't play with v0.6.7 or later and must update. Players on v0.6.13 are asked to update when the game opens; players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab ("Update to v0.6.14 - Install", or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip. The fixes below run on the host, so the host should update first.

## Story co-op

- **Objective credit goes to whoever did it.** Objectives that finish a while after the action (Aztec's shuttle launch, Dam's bungee jump and others like them) were sometimes credited to the host. They now go to the player who did the work, including when the joiner triggers a mission's ending.
- **No arrivals during an ending.** A player who joins while the mission's ending plays now waits in the lobby with "THE MISSION IS ENDING / YOU JOIN THE NEXT ONE" and comes in on the next mission, instead of dropping into a finished level.
- **A door alone is no longer cover.** On the joiner's screen, a guard's shots could be dropped as "behind cover" when only a door frame sat in the line, so the guard couldn't hurt the joiner (seen at the cell in Bunker 2). Walls and rock still count as cover.

## Versus

- **The intro swirl is smooth,** and drawn between steps above 60 fps.
- **Players face where they aim.** Another player's body no longer turns side-on while they strafe; it faces where they look with the gun forward.
- **Doors, thrown rounds, explosions and timers run every frame** online, instead of on a share of frames.
- **Sound is heard from your own Bond,** panned left and right.
- **Scorch marks stay,** with the solo debris and smoke.

## Known issues

- In Bunker 2 a joiner's punches have been reported missing the cell guard; we haven't reproduced it yet. F8 reports help.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
