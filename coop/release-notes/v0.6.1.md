Classic guards release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.5.0 to v0.6.0.** The co-op protocol version stays at 14, so v0.6.1 players can still play with anyone on v0.5.x or v0.6.0. Updating is recommended for hosts. Players on v0.6.0, v0.5.x, v0.4.0 or v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.1 - Install". Players on v0.4.0 or older must update to play with v0.5.x or later. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Smarter guards are paused

The **Smarter** guard AI is switched off for now while we finish the core of the game: keeping players in sync, the campaign, and the multiplayer mode. It is paused, not dropped. The work stays in the game's code and will come back once those are done.

- Every mission plays with the **Classic** guards, in co-op and in single player.
- The Guard AI, Guard Barks, Guard Alertness and Alert Budget options are gone from Options > GAMEPLAY > Port Options, the watch's Port Options page and the CO-OP screen's GAME SETUP. The 007 difficulty screen is back to its original four sliders.
- If you had picked Smarter before, your choice is kept in `midas.ini` but not used, so nothing changes when you update.
- A host still on v0.6.0 or v0.5.x with Smarter picked still runs Smarter guards for everyone in that game. Joiners on v0.6.1 don't see those guards' callouts. Ask the host to update.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
