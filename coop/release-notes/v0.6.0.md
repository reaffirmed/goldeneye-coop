Dynamic guards release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.5.0 to v0.5.3.** The co-op protocol version stays at 14, so v0.6.0 players can still play with anyone on v0.5.x. Updating is optional. The new guard behaviour runs on the host, so the host needs v0.6.0 to get it; joiners on v0.5.x still play fine but don't see the guards' callouts. Players on v0.5.3, v0.5.2, v0.5.1, v0.5.0, v0.4.0 or v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.0 - Install". Players on v0.4.0 or older must update to play with v0.5.x or v0.6.0. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## New: dynamic guards

All of this only happens when the host picks **Smarter** in Options > GAMEPLAY > Guard AI. It works in single player too, with your own setting. **Classic** plays exactly as before. Guards that the mission scripts control (hostages, scientists, bosses, story scenes) are left alone, and none of it can fail an objective.

- **Alert levels.** A mission starts calm. Shots, bodies and alarms raise it to alerted and then lockdown, and it settles back down when things go quiet. Alerted guards move faster and react sooner.
- **Suppression and grenades.** Guards keep a player pinned with covering fire while others move up, and throw a grenade at a player who stays behind the same cover too long.
- **Reviving is risky.** A guard that sees a player reviving a partner turns its fire on them.
- **Callouts.** Guards call out what they're doing ("NEED BACKUP!", "WHO'S THERE?", "DON'T SHOOT!") in text over their heads with a short sound. Each player can turn them off with Options > GAMEPLAY > Guard Barks.
- **Guards notice things.** Doors left open, shot-out lights and bodies, running footsteps nearby, and seeing a partner get shot all put guards on edge and send them to look.
- **Guard roles.** Some guards hang back and pick you off, some rush you, and some hold their post.
- **Wounded guards.** A badly hurt guard limps, and one left alone near a player may give up and drop his gun.
- **Falling back and cutting off.** When a fight is going badly, guards fall back to a key room and regroup. When players split up, guards move to cut off the one who's alone.
- **Alertness and alert budget.** Two new sliders, Guard Alertness and Alert Budget, are in Options > GAMEPLAY and on the 007 difficulty screen when Smarter is on. Alertness sets how far guards hear and notice things and how long they stay on edge. The budget caps how many times a mission guards may call for help, run for the alarm, throw grenades, sweep, cut you off or regroup (default 40, or No Limit). The host's settings are used in co-op.

## Fixed

- **Surface 2: guards stay in step for joiners.** The reinforcements that keep arriving on Surface 2 were also being spawned on each joiner's own copy, which could end the run with "the chrs here are not the host's". Only the host spawns them now, and joiners get them from the host.
- **Streets: civilians no longer throw joiners out of step.** The civilians that flash up for a moment on Streets were kept on the joiner for several seconds after the host dropped them. Joiners now let them go after a second.

## Known issues

- A guard running for an alarm picks it by straight-line distance, not by how far it has to walk. On Dam, the tunnel guards have no route to the nearest alarm and just keep fighting.
- After a host change, Smarter guards start their current move over rather than carrying on exactly where they were.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
