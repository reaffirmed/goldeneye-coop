Co-op fix release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.6.7 and v0.6.8.** The co-op protocol version stays at 16, so v0.6.9 players can still play with anyone on v0.6.7 or v0.6.8. Players on v0.6.6 or older can't play with v0.6.7 or later and must update. The fix needs both the host and the joiner on v0.6.9, so everyone should update. Players on v0.6.8 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.9 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Fixed

- **Guards' grenades are live on the joiner's screen.** When a guard threw a grenade, the joiner saw it drop at the guard's feet like a gun, with no fuse, and could pick it up, taking the host's live grenade along with it. Now the joiner sees the grenade thrown the way the guard faces, armed and ticking, and it can't be picked up.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
