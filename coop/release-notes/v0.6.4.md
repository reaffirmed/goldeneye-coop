Co-op fix release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.5.0 to v0.6.3.** The co-op protocol version stays at 14, so v0.6.4 players can still play with anyone on v0.5.x or v0.6.x. These fixes run on each player's own game, so every player should update, joiners most of all. Players on v0.6.3, v0.6.2, v0.6.1, v0.6.0, v0.5.x, v0.4.0 or v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.4 - Install". Players on v0.4.0 or older must update to play with v0.5.x or later. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Fixed

- **Votes work again.** A joiner who started a mission from the lobby was left out of every vote. One player's press skipped a cutscene for everyone, the host alone decided the end-of-mission vote, and a joiner could skip the intro and start playing while the host's cutscene was still running, so the host's guards couldn't be hurt. Every player now counts, so a cutscene skip needs everyone's vote again.
- **Guards and partners move smoothly above 60 fps.** At frame rates above 60, a joiner's guards and every player's partners only moved 60 times a second, so they looked choppy next to everything else. They now move every frame.
- **Partners no longer hop.** A partner's body jumped up for a frame or two each time their standing animation looped.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
