Co-op features release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.6.7 to v0.6.12.** The co-op protocol version stays at 16, so v0.6.13 players can still play with anyone on v0.6.7 or later. Players on v0.6.6 or older can't play with v0.6.7 or later and must update. Everything in this release is on your own screen, so players on different versions see their own settings. Players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.13 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Story co-op

- **A partner right in front of you fades out.** Walk into your partner and they fade smoothly from 2.6 m away and are gone by 1 m, then fade back in as you move apart, so they no longer fill your screen. Their name tag stays. Story only: versus, cutscenes and spectating are unchanged. Turn it off with **Partner Fade** in Port Options.

## Captions

- **Modern captions.** Characters' lines ("Natalya: ...") and mission messages now appear at the bottom centre like a modern shooter's subtitles: the speaker's name in colour, the line in white, on a dark box, up to three at once. **Caption Style** in Accessibility switches back to Classic for the original text. Story only.
- **Sound Captions are a list in the bottom left,** like Minecraft's subtitles: one row a sound, with an arrow on the side it came from ("v" on both sides when it's behind you), greying as the sound gets older. "Alarm" stays up while the alarm sounds. Turn them on with **Sound Captions** in Accessibility.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
