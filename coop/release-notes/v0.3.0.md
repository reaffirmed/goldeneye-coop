Feature release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.2.0 and v0.2.1.** The co-op protocol version stays at 12, so players on v0.2.x can still play with players on v0.3.0, but updating is recommended: the votes below only work when the host has v0.3.0, and the versus fix only helps players who have it. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

**This is the last release you have to download by hand.** v0.3.0 adds an in-game updater, so later releases install from the CO-OP screen. It can't update v0.2.1 or older, which have no updater: get this zip from GitHub as before.

## New

- **In-game updater.** When the game starts it checks GitHub for a newer release; nothing downloads on its own. If there is one, the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to (version) - Install". Picking it downloads the zip, checks it against GitHub's SHA-256, installs it and offers **Restart Now**. Your cart image, `midas.ini`, saves and profiles are never touched. Turn the startup check off with **Update Check** on the CONTROLS tab, or check by hand with **Check Now**.
- When a host turns you away for another co-op version, the game checks at once and offers the update, and the hint says who has which version ("Host has v0.3.1, you v0.3.0").
- **Spectator camera.** While you are down, out of lives or dropping in, Fire watches the next standing partner, Aim the one before, and Use (or B) frees the camera to fly around the level like photo mode. The HUD shows WATCHING (name) or FREE CAMERA.
- **"What next" vote after a mission.** The co-op debrief gets a third page, WHAT NEXT 3/3: Replay the mission, Next mission (after a win) or Back to the lobby, with each player's vote shown. Most votes win once everyone has voted, or 30 s after the first vote; a tie goes the host's way if the host is in it, otherwise to the lobby.
- **Cutscene skip votes.** Skipping the intro (overview and swirl) or an ending that waits for a press is now a vote: it passes when more than half the players press (2 of 2, 2 of 3, 3 of 4), and the HUD shows who it is waiting for. Playing alone, a press still skips at once.

## Fixes

- **Versus plays by the same rules on every PC.** The Friendly Fire and LTK Health options in `midas.ini` no longer apply to online versus. Before, team-mates could only hurt each other when both PCs had Friendly Fire on, and LTK Health let one player die in one hit because of their own setting. Online, team-mates now hurt each other, a team-mate kill takes a point off the team, and one-hit kills happen only in Licence to Kill. Local split-screen is unchanged.
- Every versus scenario has now been played online, including the team games (2 v 1, 2 v 2, 3 v 1), Golden Gun, Flag Tag, Licence to Kill and You Only Live Twice.
- A version refusal now shows an **Update Needed** panel; it said "Wrong Password" before.
- Replaying the same mission no longer ends the joiner's intro at once.
- While you are spectating, partners standing near your own body are drawn instead of hidden.
