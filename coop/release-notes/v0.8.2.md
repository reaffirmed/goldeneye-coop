Lobby and fixes release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.7.0 to v0.8.1.** The co-op protocol version stays at 17, so v0.8.2 players can play with anyone on v0.7.0 or later. Players on v0.6.15 or older can't play with v0.7.0 or later and must update. Players on v0.7.1 to v0.8.1 are asked when they open the game; players on v0.6.13 to v0.7.0 are asked by the game as before; players on v0.6.12 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab ("Update to v0.8.2 - Install", or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub (`GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.2-win32.zip`). Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

Who needs v0.8.2 for each change: a joiner's Ready button needs both the joiner and the host on v0.8.2, and starting alone needs the host on it. The revive and knockback fixes work when the player who goes down is on v0.8.2. Notification Size and the Facility chat fix are on each player's own game.

## Lobby

- **A Ready / Start button.** Every lobby tab now has a button at the bottom right, in both the Modern and Classic layouts. For a joiner it says **READY UP** and pulses until you're ready ("THE PARTY IS WAITING" once everyone else is). Then it turns steady green and says it's waiting for the host. Press it again to cancel. For the host it says **START**. It stays grey while anyone isn't ready ("1 PLAYER NOT READY") and pulses "EVERYONE IS READY" once they all are. START continues the campaign when there is one to continue, and otherwise opens Select Mission (Start Match in versus). Click it, point at it and press Select, or press your reload button from any tab: R on the keyboard, or X / Square / Y on a gamepad. Typing an "r" in chat doesn't press it. In Classic the list now shows 8 rows instead of 9 to make room.
- **The host can start alone.** With nobody else in the lobby, Select Mission and START now start a co-op mission, and friends who join later drop in. With partners in the lobby, they all still need to be ready first, and versus still needs a partner. While the host is alone, the party panel and Classic's photo strip show the host's own seat, not 0/4 and four open seats.

## Story co-op

- **Revives start right away.** When a partner went down, you saw "REVIVING" and a bar at once, but the bar stayed empty for about 6 seconds while their fall and fade played out. Now the bar fills from the moment they fall, so a revive takes about 2.5 seconds instead of 8.5. A fall that can't be revived (the last life with Revive or Timer) no longer offers a revive.
- **Knockback no longer strands a player.** The push from the shot that downed a player kept sliding the body along the floor. On Facility's stairs, a revive could then stand the player up under the flight above or against a ledge, with no room to move. A downed body now stays where the player fell, and revives, respawns and cutscenes stop any push still running. With Friendly Fire on, a partner's shot now pushes you the way the shot flew, not always the same way.
- **No crash when opening chat on Facility.** Pressing T to chat with a full chat history could crash the game on Facility. Opening chat shows every recent line again, and that could be more than the game had room to draw. The game now has twice the room, and if it still runs short it leaves out the oldest lines for that moment instead of crashing.

## Settings

- **Notification Size.** **Port Options > Notification Size** (50%, 75% or 100%, the default) makes the co-op notifications at the bottom left smaller: objectives done, players joining or leaving, ammo given, the host leaving. It works on top of Text Size and HUD Scale, and leaves chat, captions and the HUD as they are. It's `toast_size` under `[display]` in `midas.ini`.
- In Spanish, the settings pages' page counter no longer runs into a long title such as OPCIONES DEL PORT. All the new text is in Spanish too.

## Known issues

- The START button hasn't been tried on a host with a campaign to continue, in a lobby of 3 or 4 players, or with a real gamepad yet.
- The knockback fix was checked by watching the body stay put, not by going down on Facility's stairs themselves. If you still get stuck after a revive, an F8 report helps.
- The Facility chat fix was tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows or in a real Steam game.
- At HUD Scale High and 640x480, a 50% Notification Size is too small to read.
- The colour picker hasn't been tried with a real gamepad yet.
- The Spanish accent fix hasn't been checked on real Windows yet. If you still see gaps in Spanish words, an F8 report helps. Accents in Windows system boxes (such as the update box) haven't been checked on real Windows either.
- Adding the language files after an in-game update from v0.7.1 or older has been checked with a test of the updater, not yet with a real update. If Spanish isn't in the Language list after you update, unzip `GoldenEye-Coop-v0.8.2-win32.zip` over your game folder; your saves, settings and cart image are kept.
- The update box and its progress window were tested on Linux, not yet on real Windows. If the box doesn't show, the CO-OP screen still offers the update.
- The fix for a game splitting in two after a long dropout has been tested on our simulated network, not yet in many real Steam games. If you still end up in two separate games after a dropout, an F8 report from both players helps.
- The crash-safe saves and the updater's resume were tested on Linux. Their Windows-only parts haven't had a run on real Windows yet.
- In Bunker 2 a joiner's punches have been reported missing the cell guard; we haven't reproduced it yet. F8 reports help.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
