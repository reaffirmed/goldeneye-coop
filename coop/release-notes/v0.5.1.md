Bug-fix release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.5.0.** The co-op protocol version stays at 14, so v0.5.1 and v0.5.0 players can still play together, but updating is recommended because most of the fixes below only help players who have them. Players on v0.5.0, v0.4.0 or v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.5.1 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.4.0 or older must update to play with v0.5.x. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Fixes

- **Friendly Fire Off now covers the host's rockets.** Blasts from the host's rocket and grenade launcher rounds are tagged with their owner, so they no longer hurt joiners when friendly fire is off.
- **Drone guns aim at whoever they can see.** Each gun now turns to the nearest player in range that it can see, not only the host, and points the same way on every screen. Damage is still dealt only on the screen of the player who was hit.
- **"If I've not been seen" checks count joiners.** Guards that only a joiner had seen counted as unseen, which let unseen-only clones spawn and unseen-only script steps run. Joiners now report the guards they have seen to the host.
- **The host leaving during its ending (Dam bungee).** The new host could be left in first person or on a black screen with the mission never ending. The new host now ends the mission and everyone goes to the debrief.
- **Mission countdowns survive a host change.** A joiner who took over as host never sent the countdown, so anyone who dropped in or rejoined afterwards ran a fresh timer of their own. The new host now carries the team's countdown on.
- **The team's time survives a host change.** A late joiner who took over as host debriefed on its own, shorter mission time and could beat a target time the team didn't. The team's time now carries on from the old host's.
- **Rejoining players keep their lives,** and a player who was out comes back spectating without spending a life. A rejoined player's shots, pickups and Use also reach the host again (before, they could be dropped until the host noticed the new session).
- **No more false "slow connection" toasts after a host change.** The old host's unanswered pings no longer count as loss on the new host's link.
- **Controller chat and lobby passwords.** A player with only a controller can type with Steam's floating keyboard where Steam offers one (Big Picture, Steam Deck), or the game's on-screen keyboard otherwise.
- **The host's lobby settings are remembered** between launches (difficulty, friendly fire, host leaves, lives, bleed-out, respawn, scaling, the down rule and the versus rows), in `Data/Saves/coop_lobby.txt`.

## Checked, not reproduced

- Statue: Natalya gets up for a joiner alone and with both players beside her. INCOMPLETE at the end only happens when the helicopter countdown runs out while she is still beside it and the blast kills her, the same for the host.
- Frigate's hostage taker and Bunker 1's technician behave the same whether the joiner or the host deals with them.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files). A per-user data folder is planned as its own change.
