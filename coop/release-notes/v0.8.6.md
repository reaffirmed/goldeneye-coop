Fixes from player testing, a quick mission restart, and a simpler pause watch.

## New

- **Restart the mission.** The host's watch, Abort, now offers RESTART next to END ALL and HAND OVER. Everyone starts the same mission again without going back to the lobby.

## Watch

- Each option shows only its current value, with < > on the row you're on.
- Only the row you're on is highlighted. Rows that open a page end in >.
- Spanish: the Controls and Options rows are translated, missing letters (q, í) are back, and EFECTOS no longer runs under its volume bar.

## Fixes

- The free camera no longer gets stuck in a room or shows only sky.
- Spectating no longer draws stray tracers from the partner you watch.
- A dead player's voice is no longer placed in the world.
- Spanish objectives are no longer cut off.
- Objectives, the radar and partner status move below the dialogue bar instead of under it.
- Name tags no longer flicker at walls.
- The remote mine detonator is in the explosives group of the weapon menu.
- Pings land on the floor you point at, and pinging a civilian or unarmed person marks them.
- Egyptian: the gold gun's glass no longer opens and shuts over and over when players are apart.
- Rockets fly straight out of the launcher.
- Cradle: the joiner's guards keep up with the host's more reliably.

## Known issues

- Cradle guards out of place and an Aztec guard that wouldn't die were reported but not reproduced. F8 reports help.
- Statue's ending with Valentin as the character hasn't been looked at yet.
