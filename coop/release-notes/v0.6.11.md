Co-op and versus release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.6.7 to v0.6.10.** The co-op protocol version stays at 16, so v0.6.11 players can still play with anyone on v0.6.7 or later. Players on v0.6.6 or older can't play with v0.6.7 or later and must update. Most of these fixes need everyone in the game on v0.6.11, so everyone should update. Players on v0.6.10 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.11 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Story co-op

- **Joiners see where the host's guards' shots land.** Sparks, ricochets off walls and tracers from the guard's gun now show on every screen.
- **Guards' left-hand guns show on the joiner.**
- **Dropped guns and hats fall the same way on every screen.** Before, a joiner's copy fell upright from the guard's feet and could land a metre or more away from the host's.
- **Dead guards fade out on the joiner** the way they do on the host, instead of standing until they vanished about 5 seconds late.
- **Planes and helicopters match the host,** including for a player who drops in mid-mission.
- **A blast with no floor straight below it still goes off on every screen.** This covers a rocket that hits a cliff face or the top of a wall, or a blast in mid-air.
- **Joiners hear a guard's idle sneeze.**
- **A gadget a joiner throws that lands off the floor stays put on the host.** On Dam, a modem thrown short onto the parapet fell forever on the host's game, so the objective failed only on the joiner's screen and the two games kept correcting each other.
- **A joiner who drops in at the Dam bungee jump gets one debrief.** Before, the mission could end twice and show two debriefs.

## Versus

- **Rockets and grenade-launcher rounds are seen in flight** by every player, not just the explosion. A blast with no floor straight below it also reaches the others now.
- **Every PC shows the same end-screen stats and awards.**
- **Hit feedback:** a hit marker, an arc pointing toward whoever hit you, and a kill feed.
- **After a Match** lets the host pick Same Arena (a rematch) or Next Arena.
- **Pings go to team-mates only,** and the ping key now works more than once.
- **Public Games shows what a game is playing,** for example "Versus: Temple, Normal", and Next Match for a versus game that is in a match.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
