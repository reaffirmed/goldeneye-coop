Host sync release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.6.7 to v0.6.9.** The co-op protocol version stays at 16, so v0.6.10 players can still play with anyone on v0.6.7 or later. Players on v0.6.6 or older can't play with v0.6.7 or later and must update. Most of these fixes need both the host and the joiner on v0.6.10, so everyone should update. Players on v0.6.9 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.10 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

These fix things the host saw or heard that the joiners didn't.

## Fixed

- **The host's console and gadget presses reach the joiners' games.** Level scripts that wait for a computer, switch or gadget to be used now also react on the joiner when the host uses it. Before, effects such as the backup beeping at Dam's computer or TV screens changing only showed on the host's screen.
- **A partner driving the tank runs guards over.** Before, a joiner could drive the tank over guards and they stood there unharmed.
- **Guns a script takes from a guard disappear on the joiner too.** When a level script took a guard's gun away or swapped it, the joiner kept seeing the old gun in the guard's hand.
- **Guards' hats show on the joiner.** Hats that guards get from a script now show on the joiner too, and they fall off when shot.
- **Joiners hear guards yelp when hit, and their bodies thud when they fall.**
- **Rockets, grenade-launcher rounds, tank shells and thrown knives show in flight on every screen.** Before, other players only saw the explosion. The copies on other screens are only for show: the real shot still does the damage and the explosion.
- **Trucks and jeeps match the host.** Vehicles now stay where the host's are, and a player who drops in mid-mission sees them where they really are instead of starting from the beginning of their route.
- **Sounds from guards' and other characters' scripts play on the joiners.** On Egyptian and Train, sounds that the scripts play were only heard by the host.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
