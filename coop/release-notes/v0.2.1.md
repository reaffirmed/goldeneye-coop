Bug-fix release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.2.0.** The co-op protocol version stays at 12, so v0.2.1 and v0.2.0 players can still play together, but updating is recommended because the fixes below only help players who have them. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Fixes

- **Players far apart stay in step.** On a long or jittery line (such as players in different countries), partners and versus players no longer hitch, slide or fall out of step. Each movement track keeps more samples and the drawing delay can grow further, which stays smooth up to about 300 ms with 200 ms of jitter and 5% loss.
- Guard updates that arrive out of order no longer restart an animation, fire a burst again or flip a guard's hidden or dying state.
- A joiner's own objectives wait for the host's round trip before they count, so they don't flicker on a slow line.
- A burst of game events now catches up quickly on a slow line instead of draining one packet per round trip, and the games no longer bounce empty acknowledgements every tick.
- Guards aim at a far-away joiner with up to 400 ms of lag compensation (was 250 ms).
- After host migration, each player's clock and the guard tracks move to the right player.

## New

- **Connection readout.** In the lobby party panel, each joiner's seat shows signal bars and the round trip beside the name, plus "N% LOSS" when packets are lost.
- In a mission, a partner on a fair or poor line shows the round trip (and loss) after their name on the HUD.
- A line that stays poor for 4 seconds shows a "<name> - slow connection" toast, at most once a minute.
