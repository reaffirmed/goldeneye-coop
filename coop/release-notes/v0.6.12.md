Co-op fixes release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.6.7 to v0.6.11.** The co-op protocol version stays at 16, so v0.6.12 players can still play with anyone on v0.6.7 or later. Players on v0.6.6 or older can't play with v0.6.7 or later and must update. Most of these fixes need everyone in the game on v0.6.12, so everyone should update. Players on v0.6.11 back to v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.6.12 - Install" (or pick **Check Now** on the CONTROLS tab). Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Story co-op

- **A player who rejoins after a crash can play again.** Before, everything a rejoined player sent the host was dropped for the rest of the mission: presses, pickups, throws and mines going off.
- **A player who drops in or rejoins sees what is already lying about.** Mines, a planted modem or bug, and guns dropped by guards that spawned mid-mission were missing for them.
- **After the host leaves, your own mines are still yours.** Before, the new host's watch set off the old host's mines and couldn't set off its own.
- **Guards see and shoot a partner driving the tank.** On Runway they used to ignore a joiner in the tank or run around in front of it.
- **A joiner's tank shells, rockets and grenade-launcher rounds hurt guards again.** v0.6.11 had stopped sending them to the host.
- **Surface 1's respawning guards show on the joiner.** A respawned guard could get no copy on the joiner, or start with an old guard's state. An F8 report now also says why a guard isn't drawn, if it happens again.
- **A mine stuck to a door lands in the same place for everyone,** and a partner's mines are no longer deleted when the game runs short of room for thrown items.
- **A parked tank is in the same place for a player who joins later.**

## Discord

- **Discord shows what you are playing, and friends can Ask to Join from Discord.** It is now on for everyone. If Discord isn't running, the game carries on without it.

## Known issues

- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
