First release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

Two to four players play the solo missions together over Steam, or play versus. Nobody opens ports or types IP addresses. Every player needs this same zip (co-op protocol version 11) and their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

This is the same code as the last play-tested build (commit 4739443, the character select build with the third-player and Statue fixes).

## Features

- **Story co-op for 2-4 players over Steam**, hosted from the new CO-OP menu, with invites to Steam friends, public games with an optional password, and guards scaled up for 3-4 players.
- **Character select** in the lobby: pick any multiplayer character or Mission Bond from a film-strip picker. Looks only; everyone plays the same.
- **Online versus** (multiplayer) over Steam.
- **Shared world**: pickups, thrown items, guard gun drops, keycards, knocked-over objects, Use, doors and explosions are the same on every screen.
- **Netcode**: every setup object replicated from the host with a join snapshot and periodic resync, a desync checksum with automatic correction, smooth movement between packets, prediction for a joiner's own shots and lag compensation for guards' shots.
- **Hold-Use revive**, cutscenes in sync for everyone, and a team results screen with your own stats.
- **Ping key** to mark a spot or a guard for every partner.
- **Positional voice chat** over Steam Voice, with per-player mutes and a TALKING tag.
- **Text chat, spectate, photo mode** and more from the feature batch.
- **Drop-in** to a game in progress, and **host migration**: if the host leaves or crashes, another player takes over at once.
- **Steam rich presence and invites from anywhere**, plus Discord presence and Ask to Join.
- **Presentation**: loading, reconnect and error screens, objective toasts bottom left with credit for the player who did it, partner audio, controller button prompts, a lobby with photo cards for the players and the next mission, and lobby scrolling with wheel, keys and pad.
- **Crash reports** and an F8 bug report zipped to the Desktop, co-op saves, and accessibility options (sound captions, text size).
- Friendly Fire lobby option; auto-aim never locks onto a partner.

## Fixes since the play tests

- A joiner no longer crashes when the host sets off the Statue ambush (an unarmed Trevelyan).
- A player who leaves and joins again is no longer stuck alone in the lobby.
- A joiner's door switch no longer shuts the gate it opened (Dam tunnel gates).
- Guard gun drops reach the joiner on the floor, keys included, and lie in the same place and angle on every screen.
- Bodies no longer block the joiner, and helmet or gun hits and punches no longer cause false deaths.
- The host's watch Abort works again.
- Shooting a partner no longer crashes the game.
- A fall the team got over no longer fails your debrief.
