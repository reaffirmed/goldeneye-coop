Guard AI and menu fix release of GoldenEye 007 story co-op, built on Project Midas 1.0.2b.

**Works with v0.5.0, v0.5.1 and v0.5.2.** The co-op protocol version stays at 14, so v0.5.3 players can still play with anyone on v0.5.x. Updating is optional, but recommended for anyone who hosts 3 or 4 player games with Smarter guards. Players on v0.5.2, v0.5.1, v0.5.0, v0.4.0 or v0.3.0 get the update from the game itself: the top of the CO-OP screen's PLAY tab shows "Update to v0.5.3 - Install". Players on v0.4.0 or older must update to play with v0.5.x. Players on v0.2.1 or older have no updater and need this zip from GitHub. Every player needs their own GoldenEye 007 (USA) cart image in the `Data` folder; the image is not included. See `README-COOP.txt` inside the zip.

## Fixed

- **Accessibility can be picked in Options > GAMEPLAY.** The fourth row, Accessibility, sat just below where the crosshair could reach, so mouse, stick and keys all stopped at Port Options. The four rows are now spaced a little closer so every one can be selected. This also applies to All Options on the CO-OP screen.
- **Smarter guards hide from every player.** With 3 or 4 players, a guard taking cover only hid from the player it was fighting, so it often crouched in full view of someone else. Cover now has to be out of sight of every player the guard could fight. Guards take cover just as often. This only matters when the host picks **Smarter** guard AI, and it needs the host (or whoever takes over as host) to be on v0.5.3.

## Known issues

- A guard running for an alarm picks it by straight-line distance, not by how far it has to walk. On Dam, the tunnel guards have no route to the nearest alarm and just keep fighting.
- After a host change, Smarter guards start their current move over rather than carrying on exactly where they were.
- The game still keeps its saves and updates in its own folder, so it needs a folder you can write to (not Program Files).
